MSP3520 SPI clock probe - flashable firmware
============================================
ESP32-S3 (esp32s3) - ESP-IDF v6.1.0 - built 2026-11-15
Default partition table, tiny app (221 KB), 8 MB flash setting
(DevKitC-1 N8 / N8R2 / N16 all work).

WHAT THIS IS
  A one-shot diagnostic that answers, on your actual board:
  1. Which SPI clocks the ESP32-S3 GPSPI can actually produce
     (the driver's own answer via spi_device_get_actual_freq), and
  2. whether a "1-bit I80 LCD_CAM mode" exists (it does not - the
     probe calls esp_lcd_new_i80_bus(bus_width=1) and prints the
     ESP_ERR_INVALID_ARG rejection).
  It also measures the real bus clock by timing dummy 16 KiB
  transfers, and dumps the live SPI2 clock register.

  SAFE WITH THE DISPLAY WIRED AND POWERED: the probe holds CS/DC/RST
  high, so the ILI9488 ignores every clock wiggle. Nothing on the
  screen changes; when done, re-flash a video firmware to get the
  display back.

  Already verified in emulation (QEMU): phase 1 prints
    request 10 -> 10.0, 15 -> 16.0, 20 -> 20.0, 25/30 -> 26.67,
    35..60 -> 40.0, 60.001..80 -> 80.0
  and the i80 rejections. On hardware you additionally get the
  measured-throughput column (phase 2 needs real DMA, which the
  emulator does not model - that is why phase 2 only completes on
  the real chip).

FILES
  bootloader.bin              flash at 0x0
  partition-table.bin         flash at 0x8000
  msp3520_clock_probe.bin     flash at 0x10000
  msp3520_clock_probe_full.bin all three merged - flash at 0x0 (easiest)

FLASH IT (pick your COM port):
  C:\Espressif\tools\python\v6.1\venv\Scripts\python.exe -m esptool --chip esp32s3 -p COM10 -b 460800 --before default-reset --after hard-reset write-flash 0x0 msp3520_clock_probe_full.bin
  (flashing takes seconds - no video embedded)

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE (console, either USB port, 115200):
  === MSP3520 SPI clock probe (ESP32-S3, IDF v6.1) ===
  ... phase 1 table (request vs the driver's actual clock) ...
  esp_lcd_new_i80_bus(bus_width=1) -> ESP_ERR_INVALID_ARG
  ... phase 2 table (live register + measured MHz) ...
  === VERDICT ... ===
  probe complete - safe to power off.

WIRING (unchanged - it only reads SCK/MOSI pins 11/12 and holds
CS/DC/RST/BL): CS GPIO10, DC GPIO9, RESET GPIO14, MOSI GPIO11,
SCK GPIO12, backlight GPIO47.

MSP3520 video player v6 (FIELD-INTERLACED - double-cadence build)
=======================================================================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  bootloader.bin                flash to 0x0
  partition-table.bin           flash to 0x8000
  msp3520_video_oc_test.bin     flash to 0x10000  (app + embedded video)
  msp3520_video_oc_test_full.bin  all three merged - flash to 0x0

FLASH (wiring unchanged from v5: SCK=12 MOSI=11 MISO=13 CS=10 DC=9
RESET=14 LED=47)
  idf.py -p COMx flash            (from the msp3520-video-oc-test project)
  or: python -m esptool --chip esp32s3 -b 460800 write-flash 0x0 \
        bootloader.bin 0x8000 partition-table.bin 0x10000 \
        msp3520_video_oc_test.bin
  or: python -m esptool --chip esp32s3 -b 460800 write-flash 0x0 \
        msp3520_video_oc_test_full.bin

WHAT IT DOES (v6 = the frame-rate interlace you asked for)
  The video is stored and played as FIELDS: each field is one 240-px
  half of the screen carrying the source frame at its OWN timestamp
  (halves alternate left/right, 173 fields from the 119-frame clip).
  At 40 MHz the wire carries ~21.7 fields/s - double the ~10.85 full
  frames/s of the progressive builds - and because every field carries
  a NEWER source sample, motion advances at that doubled cadence.
  No PSRAM needed: works on every board (N8, N8R2, N16R8 ...).

  EXPECTED ARTIFACTS (the interlace trade you accepted): a stationary
  vertical seam at mid-screen; during fast horizontal pans the two
  halves shear by ~46 ms of motion. Static scenes are pixel-identical
  to the progressive build. Anything beyond that (snow, torn stripes,
  frozen picture, garbage blocks) is a fault: press n.

  1. Boots, reads the controller ID, fills the screen dark blue.
  2. Sweeps the video at 10..80 MHz in 5 MHz steps, 10 s per step.
     Keys DURING playback (single keypress, no Enter):
     y = clean   n = artifacts   r = restart   q = quit sweep
     d = cycle SCK/MOSI drive capability (80 MHz experiment)
  3. Summary table (fields/s per MHz), retest loop (type MHz + Enter),
     then continuous playback mode (type MHz + Enter).

A/B AGAINST PROGRESSIVE
  Flash the v5.1 package (msp3520-video-firmware-v5) to compare: same
  clip, same sweep, progressive full frames at ~10.5 fps / 40 MHz with
  zero artifacts. v6 doubles the cadence at the cost of the seam.

EMBED YOUR OWN CLIP
  python3 tools/convert_video.py YOURCLIP.mp4 main/video.bin \
      --scan field-halves
  (README section 2; a >= 21.7 fps source shows the full 2x gain)

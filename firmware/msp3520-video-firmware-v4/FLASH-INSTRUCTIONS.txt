MSP3520 video player v4.1 (adds the 80 MHz experiment hotkey) - flashable firmware
ESP32-S3 (esp32s3) - ESP-IDF v6.1 - 2026-11-15
THE FPS FIX: v3 sent every display line as 4 separate SPI transactions
(1280 per frame, ~12 ms/frame of pure dead time - why you measured
8.5 fps instead of ~10.5). v4 sets the full-screen window once, then
streams each frame as ONE RAMWR + 20 back-to-back 16-line DMA blocks
(41 transactions per frame). Expect ~10.5 fps at the 35-60 MHz steps.
Also: interlacing is gone - no more combing on motion.

  msp3520_video_oc_test.bin      flash at 0x10000  (7.1 MB - video inside)
  msp3520_video_oc_test_full.bin all three merged - flash at 0x0 (easiest)

WHAT TO EXPECT (S3 clock reality: 35-60 MHz requests run at 40 MHz,
65-80 MHz requests run at 80 MHz - no other steps exist):
  - boot: dark blue fill, banner, ID line, sweep from 10 MHz
  - steps 10-30 MHz: lower fps, clean picture
  - steps 35-60 MHz (all = 40 MHz actual): ~10.5 fps  <- the v4 gain
  - steps 65-80 MHz (all = 80 MHz actual): expect snow. This is not your
    wiring: the ILI9488 datasheet specifies a 50 ns minimum serial write
    cycle (20 MHz max) - 40 MHz is already 2x over spec (the highest
    reliably reported clock for this controller anywhere), 80 MHz is 4x.
    Last $0 experiment if you want to try anyway:
      1. series resistor 47-100 ohm on SCK at the ESP32 end (dampens
         reflections on the jumper wires), GND wire routed next to SCK
      2. NEW in v4.1: press d during playback - it cycles the SCK/MOSI
         GPIO drive capability 0/1/2/3 live (weaker edges ring less).
         Try each setting while the 80 MHz step plays.
      3. retest at 70/75/80 with the firmware's retest loop
    Expectation is low - the die is the limiter, not the wires.

IF COLORS LOOK WRONG: red/blue swapped -> LCD_MADCTL 0x28 -> 0x20 in
main/msp3520.h and rebuild. Upside-down -> 0xE8. (0x28 is hardware-
verified on your panel, so this should not be needed.)

WIRING (unchanged): CS GPIO10, DC GPIO9, RESET GPIO14, MOSI GPIO11,
SCK GPIO12, backlight GPIO47, VCC 3.3-5V, GND.

THE HONEST CEILING ON THIS PANEL (ILI9488 over SPI):
  40 MHz bus -> 10.8 fps theoretical, ~10.5 real (this firmware)
  80 MHz bus -> 20.7 fps theoretical, but the ILI9488 dies at 80
  24 fps     -> needs 88.5 Mbps; impossible on ILI9488 over SPI
  16-bit color -> not supported by the ILI9488 on native SPI at all
  (16-bit SPI only exists on modules with a board-level SPI->parallel
  converter, e.g. KeDei v4.0, or other controllers like ST7796)
